# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Eivar Online players who use the web frontend to discover the game, follow updates, manage their account, and enter the MMO. Authentication surfaces should let returning and new players complete their task quickly without leaving the world’s visual language.

## Product Purpose

Eivar Online extends the Bevy MMO with a polished web surface for account access, news, game information, and related player-facing workflows. Success means functional flows remain immediately understandable while feeling like part of the same ancient magical world as the game.

## Brand Personality

Imposing, ancient, and mystical. The interface should feel crafted from dark blue stone and metal, burnished gold, and restrained cyan magic rather than assembled from generic web components.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS styling, glass cards, flat browser-default presentation, unrelated modern branding, overcrowded auth pages, and decorative UI that conflicts with the supplied Eivar component artwork. Do not approximate supplied controls in CSS when an intentional matching asset exists.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the game world across every player-facing surface.
2. Keep familiar interactions semantic and immediately usable beneath the fantasy presentation.
3. Use the supplied component artwork as the canonical visual vocabulary.
4. Let major fantasy artwork breathe instead of filling every space with copy or controls.
5. Keep decoration subordinate to legibility, focus, and task completion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic HTML, visible labels, keyboard operation, strong focus indication, useful autofill metadata, readable contrast, announced validation errors, and reduced-motion compatibility. Decorative raster assets must not replace the native semantics of form controls.
