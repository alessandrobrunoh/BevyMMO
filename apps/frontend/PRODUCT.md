# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fantasy and MMORPG players discovering Eivar Online, following its development, reading news and updates, consulting the wiki, and joining the community. They need an immediately recognizable world identity and clear access to the site's primary destinations.

## Product Purpose

Present Eivar Online as an evolving fantasy MMORPG and make its world, systems, development progress, editorial content, and community easy to discover. Success means the website feels like an authentic extension of the game rather than a generic web application.

## Brand Personality

Ancient, adventurous, mystical, bright, immersive, premium without luxury excess, and modern enough to remain clear and readable.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS landing page, Bootstrap dashboard, dark World of Warcraft clone, black-and-red hardcore RPG site, futuristic sci-fi interface, ornate parchment site, or mobile-game cash shop. Avoid excessive neon, glassmorphism, generic rounded cards, and decorative clutter that competes with the world artwork.

## Design Principles

1. Make the world, not the web interface, the protagonist.
2. Use runes, metalwork, texture, and glow as recognizable accents with restraint.
3. Balance cinematic fantasy presentation with clear contemporary navigation.
4. Communicate development status honestly without invented dates or player metrics.
5. Preserve visual identity and legibility across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic landmarks and controls, visible keyboard focus, sufficient text contrast, descriptive accessible names, keyboard-operable navigation, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. Decorative art must not pollute the accessibility tree.
